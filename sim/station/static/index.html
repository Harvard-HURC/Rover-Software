    Fly and Map are inspection views that only the simulation has: at URC the operators see only what the rover sends. In them the keyboard flies the fly camera or moves the map, and the rover holds still unless a gamepad drives it. Orthographic pictures have no cast shadows, and perspective ones none from more than about 500 m away.

    The rover eye sits at the rover's camera, where its pan and tilt axes meet, and only turns there. The rover's camera head turns with it, so the onboard camera sees what you see (except while released to autonomy).

    The rover stops when this page stops sending for 0.5 s: closing the tab or clicking elsewhere stops it. Pressing Cmd (for a browser shortcut) lets go of the keys you hold. While you drive, the status LED shows blue (teleoperation, URC rule 1.e.ii).
